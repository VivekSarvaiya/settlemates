## 1. The calculator

The calculator runs entirely in your browser. The names and amounts you type are used to work out the split on your device, and they're cleared when you close or refresh the page.

## 2. Shared links

When you copy or share a split, the names and amounts are packed into the link itself, after the # sign. Browsers don't send that part of a link to any server, so we never receive it. Anyone you give the link to can open it and see the split, so share it only with the people involved.

## 3. Hosting

The site is hosted by Vercel. Like any web host, it processes the technical details needed to deliver pages, such as your IP address, browser type and the page you asked for, and may keep them briefly in server logs for security and reliability. We don't use these logs to identify you.

## 4. Fonts and other services

Fonts are served from this site, not from Google or another font service, and the site doesn't load third-party scripts.

## 5. If you email us

We use your email address and message only to reply to you. We won't add you to a mailing list.

## 6. Changes to this policy

If anything here changes, for example if we add analytics or ads, we'll update this page and its date before the change goes live.
