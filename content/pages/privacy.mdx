## 1. The calculator

The calculator runs entirely in your browser. The names and amounts you type are used to work out the split on your device, and they're cleared when you close or refresh the page.

## 2. Shared links

When you copy or share a split, the names and amounts are packed into the link itself, after the # sign. Browsers don't send that part of a link to any server, so we never receive it, and our analytics is set up to leave it out too. Anyone you give the link to can open it and see the split, so share it only with the people involved.

## 3. Analytics

We use Vercel Web Analytics and Vercel Speed Insights, from the company that hosts the site, to understand how SettleMates is used and how fast it loads. They record things like the page you visited, the site or search that brought you here, your approximate location (country, and sometimes region or city, worked out from your IP address), your device, operating system and browser, and how quickly the page loaded. They never receive the names or amounts in a split: the part of the address after the # sign is removed before anything is sent.

Vercel Web Analytics doesn't use third-party cookies. To count unique visitors, it creates a hash from your request and discards it after 24 hours. Speed Insights records its measurements anonymously, without linking them to you or your IP address. We don't use analytics to find out who you are, and we don't sell what it records.

## 4. Hosting

The site is hosted by Vercel. Like any web host, it processes the technical details needed to deliver pages, such as your IP address, browser type and the page you asked for, and may keep them briefly in server logs for security and reliability. We don't use these logs to identify you.

## 5. Fonts and other services

Fonts are served from this site, not from Google or another font service. Apart from analytics, the site doesn't load third-party scripts.

## 6. If you email us

We use your email address and message only to reply to you. We won't add you to a mailing list.

## 7. Changes to this policy

If anything here changes, for example if we add ads, we'll update this page and its date before the change goes live.
