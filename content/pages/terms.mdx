## 1. What SettleMates is

A free tool that works out how to split shared costs and who should pay whom. It doesn't hold, send or receive money, and it isn't a bank, a payment service or a financial adviser. SettleMates is run by an individual, the founder named on the [About page](/about).

## 2. Check before you pay

We work hard to get the maths right, but results depend on the numbers you enter. Check amounts before you pay anyone or ask anyone to pay you.

## 3. Shared links

Anyone with a link you share can see the names and amounts in it. Only share links with the people involved.
